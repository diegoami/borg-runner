What it would do differently

The leads the player wrote, each with where it happened and what would settle it. An ask is a link into the replay above.

  1. A Paladin's first cast of Bless is 4 experience and a level, and it is the cheapest experience in the game.

    Evidence: class.txt gives spell:Bless:1:2:20:4 — the last field is the experience the first cast pays — and the cast at ask 90 took me from level 3 to level 4 with 5 points to spare ("Welcome to level 4", and the sidebar's NXT went from 36 to 30).

    How to test: a Paladin run that casts Bless at level 1 and counts the experience: the same four points at level 1 would be a third of the way to level 2.

    ask 90 · turn 1,512

  2. A single flask of oil killed Grip, a 25 HP AC 36 unique, at range 1.

    Evidence: ask 51, one throw, "Grip, Farmer Maggot's Dog, dies", and the level jumped 1 → 3 (60 experience). This is the single most valuable measurement in this run, because 60 experience is about fifteen depth-1 kills.

    How to test: throw oil at an AC 36 monster from range 1 and count the hit rate over ten throws.

    ask 51 · turn 691

  3. A Paladin's throw skill beats an AC 24 monster at range 2.

    Evidence: the salamander (14 HP, AC 24) died to one flask (ask 98), the kobold (8 HP, AC 24) took two (one missed, ask 101–102).

    How to test: the same test with a recorded hit rate, and with the sheet's to-hit beside it, so the formula in §5 of the manual can be corrected against the code in player-attack.c.

    ask 98, 101-102 · turn 1,612-1,672

  4. The grey mushroom patch's spores reach one tile diagonally, and the cure is a potion, not a fight.

    Evidence: asks 54–62 (the spore message with the patch one tile south-east of me; four asks of hitting walls and a locked door while confused; the Cure Light Wounds curing it and giving twenty turns of immunity, after which one deliberate attack killed it).

    How to test: stand one tile diagonally from a patch on a fresh level and count the hits over ten turns.

    ask 54-62 · turn 711-761

  5. A character that cannot dig is walled off by veins, and the map does not say so.

    Evidence: the pocket at the south of my region ends in a vein wall (%) that stopped a chain of eight steps (ask 136), and class.txt gives the Paladin skill-dig:0:0, so my digging skill of about 7 cannot clear magma (needs more than 10) or quartz (more than 20).

    How to test: a Paladin on a level with a treasure vein: does the vein stop the level, and does the map mark it as a wall or as floor?

    ask 136 · turn 3,874

  6. The game kills the process and the run's files are still good.

    This run's game was killed by a machine restart with the character alive and the last eleven asks (the report, the record, the archive) written afterwards from the run's own files.

    How to test: the next run that loses its game should be finished the same way — borg_runner stop writes the record even when it reports "no game of its own is running", and keep, archive and audit all work from the files.
