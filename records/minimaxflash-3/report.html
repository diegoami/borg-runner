What it would do differently

The leads the player wrote, each with where it happened and what would settle it. An ask is a link into the replay above.

  1. Three or four down staircases per level, each at a corridor end.

    Settled by counting > in the M view of several levels; if a level really holds three or four, "the down staircase is unfindable" is much weaker than the repository assumes, and a corridor end is a shape you can walk to.

  2. Magic Mapping's window.

    Settled by buying one (65 gold), reading it on depth 1, and looking for a > in the 44×80 window the M view then shows.

  3. The Word of Recall's tick.

    Settled by reading it and counting the turns until the yank: the source says 150-350, and the practical consequence is that it is not an escape from a fight.

  4. Gold seams.

    Any class that can dig (a Ranger? a Mage with a shovel?) turns every * on a level into a gold pile worth about 100. Worth one run by a class that can dig.

  5. A Warrior cannot dig.

    skill-dig:0:0 is in class.txt; the consequence for every Warrior run is that a treasure vein is a landmark and never a source of gold.
