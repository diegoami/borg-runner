- SOURCE ANSWER to the repo's standing question 'is the down staircase findable at all': NO SPATIAL RULE EXISTS. gen-cave.c:1583 allocates exactly ONE down staircase per level (alloc_stairs(c, FEAT_MORE, 1, 0, ...)), gen-util.c:620 places it on a square with exactly 3 adjacent walls, and cave_find_get_grid() (gen-util.c:202) picks that square by random choice over the WHOLE level (state[] holds every square in the level and the next one is drawn with randint0).
