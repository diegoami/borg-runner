- THE WALK IS SHORT BECAUSE THE GROUND BETWEEN IS NOT KNOWN (ask 117): on a fresh level only the square I stand on, the squares the detection spell memorised (the doors and the stairs) and the few tiles my torch lights are known. find_path's only_known test needs BOTH ends known and a path over remembered ground, and there is none between me and a staircase 15 tiles north, so the walk is one tile. The piece that works is plain digit walking: a digit string stops only at a WALL, not at a turn, so eight '8's in one ask cover eight tiles of corridor if the corridor runs straight, and the message line says how far it got. (ask 117)
- WHERE THE STAIRCASE IS, FROM THE M MAP (ask 118): the M map's boxes are 2.5 tiles wide and 3 tiles high (78 boxes for 198 tiles, 22 for 66), my '@' is at box (14,12), so I stand at level (35,36) and the level's down staircases are at level (23,21) and (66,12) -- twelve west and fifteen north of me, and thirty east and twenty-four north. The nearer one is four rows above the top of my panel, and the corridor north of my square is a wall, so the way there is a loop: south, west, then north. And the walking has to be done with digit strings, because the pathfinder's path is computed over remembered ground and a fresh level has almost none. (ask 118)
- THE MONSTER DENSITY, MEASURED (asks 116-122): the level has z_info->level_monster_min + randint1(8) + k monsters, about fifty, on 13,068 squares -- one per 250. My torch shows a 3x3 patch, so to sweep the level I would have to walk about 260 squares, and my best walking rate is about eight tiles an ask, so a search for one kill is thirty asks. That is the real bottleneck of this run, and it is not the fighting: the fighting is cheap (a flask of oil is 9 to 36 damage and a giant green frog on depth 2 dies to one or two), the FINDING is expensive. The Find Traps spell is worth more for its doors than for its stairs, because a door is a map of where the rooms are, and a room is a hundred squares of concentrated search. (ask 122)
