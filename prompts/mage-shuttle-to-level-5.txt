A brief: can a mage reach character level 5 on depth 1 and the town alone?

This is an objective to add to PLAYGAME.txt, not a replacement for it. Read
PLAYGAME.txt and follow it, with these substitutions, and read this brief's
objective in place of its "about 150 decisions" rule.

    run name    {RUNNAME}
    seed        {SEED}, which no record in records/OVERVIEW.md may already use
    family      {AGENT}, one of minimax, sonnet, codex, qwen, glm

== THE OBJECTIVE, WHICH REPLACES THE 150-DECISION RULE ==

Get the character to **character level 5** by shuttling between the town and
depth 1, and go no deeper than depth 1.

The page calls this the town-and-depth-1 shuttle and says what it is worth:
the loop is two keys, because the town puts you on its down staircase and
depth 1 puts you on an up staircase; every `>` builds a new depth 1; and the
shuttle itself pays nothing, because stairs give no experience. What pays is
in section 1, and it is the whole of this experiment: a kill pays
`mexp x monster level / your level`, so the same monster is worth a quarter as
much at CL4 as at CL1, and CL5 is 70 experience for a Human and 84 for a
Gnome or a Half-Troll. Do the arithmetic for the race you drew before you
start, off the page, and write it in your first note.

Three things the page says that decide how you spend your mana:

- studying a spell costs one action and nothing else, so take every slot the
  moment the status line offers it;
- the first cast of each spell pays experience - a mage's five CL1 and CL2
  spells pay 18 between them, for the price of the mana;
- Magic Missile is 3d4, three to twelve damage, from CL1 to CL5, and has no
  damage bonus. `agents/minimax/MAGE.md` said otherwise until 2026-11-10 and
  now carries a note about it: if you meet another number like that, correct
  the line where it stands and say in your report what it cost you.

Budget: up to **300 decisions**. Stop as soon as the sidebar says level 5.
Running out of asks short of it is a result, and so is dying; both answer the
question better than a tidy story. Do not go to depth 2 to make the
experience come faster - the point is to price depth 1.

Report, as numbers: cycles made, asks per character level, which kills paid
and which stopped paying, how many asks went to resting and shopping rather
than fighting, and - if you stopped short - what you would need to finish.
