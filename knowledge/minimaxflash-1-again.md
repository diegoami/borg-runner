Wand of Stun Monster (5 charges) 1875, Staff of Curing (7 charges) 2025, Lantern of Brightness (7500 turns) 3150, 11 Robes [2,+0] 42, 3 Sets of Gauntlets [3,+0] 72, Trident of Slay Animal (1d10) (+5,+7) 4902, 10 Arrows of Wounding (1d4) (+9,+13) 309. NO Deep Descent and NO Magic Mapping. The reason is in the source: store_create_random() in store.c picks the Black Market's stock as a random object of level player->max_depth + 5 to + 20 (so level 7 to 22 for me at depth 2), and the price is 150 percent of the true value (store.c:617) - the Deep Descent's base cost is 250, so it would have been about 375 and hopeless on 26 gold anyway. The cheapest useful thing there is a robe of +2 AC for 42 gold. (ask 384)
- ALCHEMIST STOCK ON SEED 23, LATER IN THE DAY - and it answers the previous run's open question: 'Magic Mapping 65 g did not help' and 'the Alchemist did not stock one this seed'. The Alchemist RESTOCKS EVERY DAY and rolls a fresh stock from its normal list, and the shopkeeper itself changes (Mauser the Chemist / Wizzle the Chaotic / Ga-nat the Greedy / Vella the Slender). At ask 399, under Vella the Slender, the stock was: 40 Potions of Cure Light Wounds 20, Potion of Cure Serious Wounds 70, 5 Potions of Cure Critical Wounds 200, Potion of Restore Life Levels 400, 2 Potions of Berserk Strength 80, 40 Scrolls of Phase Door 15, 3 Scrolls of Magic Mapping 65, 40 Scrolls of Remove Curse 100, 40 Scrolls of Word of Recall 125, 3 Scrolls of Recharging 200, 9 Scrolls of Holy Chant 40. SO MAGIC MAPPING IS FOR SALE - three of them - for 65 gold. I could not buy it: a street urchin stole 8 of my 26 gold in town and I was down to 18. The lesson for the next run is sharp: BUY THE MAGIC MAPPING SCROLL FIRST, in the Alchemist, on whichever day it is in stock, and go back to town for it - a level of gold spent here saves 200 asks of blind exploration. The stock rolls when the game day changes (store.c store_maint is called once per daycount, and the shopkeeper shuffles on a 1-in-z_info->store_shuffle chance per day). (ask 399)
